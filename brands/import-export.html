About IGO Exports & Imports

IGO Import & Export is a B2B managed trade desk based in Chennai providing end-to-end agri-commodity export/import services: verified sourcing, third-party quality inspection (SGS/Bureau Veritas), export documentation and global freight/logistics for buyers of spices, cereals, seeds, fertilizers, nursery plants and farm machinery.
